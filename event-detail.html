Bristol Balloon Fiesta

A spectacular week of hot air balloons and family entertainment

📅 Date: 15-22 February 2026 (7 days)
📍 Venue: Ashton Court Estate, Bristol
🎟️ Capacity: 300 attendees
✅ Available Tickets: 150 remaining
⏰ Last Booking Date: 10 February 2026

About This Event

Join us for the spectacular Bristol Balloon Fiesta, a week-long celebration featuring dozens of colorful hot air balloons taking to the skies. This family-friendly event includes balloon rides, food stalls, live music, and entertainment.

Experience the magic of mass ascents at dawn and dusk, enjoy thrilling balloon rides (additional fee), and explore vendor markets featuring local artisans and food producers.

Event Highlights:

  • Mass balloon ascents twice daily
  • Tethered balloon rides available
  • Live music and entertainment stages
  • Family activities and kids' zones
  • Local food and craft vendors
  • Evening night glow displays

Important Information:

  • Event runs rain or shine
  • Suitable for all ages
  • Parking available on-site (£5)
  • Student discount: 10% off with valid ID
