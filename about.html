About Bristol Community Events

Bringing Bristol together through amazing events

Our Mission

Bristol Community Events (BCE) is dedicated to promoting tourism and community engagement in Bristol by providing a unified platform for discovering and booking the city's diverse range of events. From exhibitions and workshops to sports events, musical performances, and religious celebrations, we bring all of Bristol's vibrant cultural offerings under one roof.

🎯

Our Vision

To be Bristol's premier event management system, connecting residents and visitors with unforgettable experiences across the city.

🤝

Community First

We prioritize accessibility and inclusivity, offering student discounts and ensuring events are available to all members of our community.

🌟

Quality Events

We carefully curate and partner with the best venues and event organizers to ensure exceptional experiences for our users.

What We Offer

Easy Event Discovery

Browse through hundreds of events across multiple categories including exhibitions, workshops, sports, musical performances, theatre, and community celebrations.

Simple Booking Process

Our streamlined booking system makes it easy to purchase tickets, with support for single and multi-day events, and automated discount calculations.

Flexible Ticketing

Book tickets up to 2 months in advance and benefit from early bird discounts. Students enjoy a permanent 10% discount on all events.

Secure Transactions

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information and payment details.

Commitment to Accessibility

We believe events should be accessible to everyone. That's why we offer:

  • 10% student discount on all events (with valid student ID)
  • Early bird discounts up to 20% for advance bookings
  • Free events across various categories
  • Clear booking information with no hidden fees
  • Flexible cancellation policies
  • Waiting list system for popular events
