Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book tickets?

Simply browse our events, select the event you're interested in, and click "Book Tickets". You'll need to create an account or login to complete your booking.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, cancellations are possible. Bookings cancelled 40+ days in advance incur no charges. Between 25-39 days: 40% fee. Within 25 days: 100% of booking price is charged.

How do I get the student discount?

Register as a student when creating your account. You'll need to show your valid student ID at the event entrance to receive the 10% discount.

What are early bird discounts?

Book 50-60 days in advance for 20% off, 35-50 days for 15% off, 25-35 days for 10% off, and 15-25 days for 5% off.
